# Mr Nathan's Maths Tuition — Down-Up Subtraction

## What is it?

**Down-Up Subtraction** is an interactive web app that teaches children aged 5–7 to subtract 9, 8, 7 and 6 from any two-digit number — quickly, confidently, and entirely in their head.

It is the companion product to **Mr Nathan's Up-Down Addition**, and together they form a complete mental arithmetic system built around a single powerful insight: near-ten numbers have a pattern, and once a child sees it, they never forget it.

---

## The Down-Up Method

Subtracting 9 is almost the same as subtracting 10 — you just need to add 1 back. The Down-Up method makes this visible and memorable.

When you subtract 9 from a two-digit number, split the number into its two digits. The **tens digit goes Down by 1** (subtracting 10) and the **ones digit goes Up by 1** (adding back 1). The result appears instantly.

Note: the method works cleanly when the ones digit of the number being subtracted from is **less than** the subtractor — so the ones digit never overflows past 9. Questions where this wouldn't hold are simply not included in the app.

- **14 − 9**: tens 1→0, ones 4→5 → answer: **5**
- **23 − 8**: tens 2→1, ones 3→5 → answer: **15**
- **35 − 7**: tens 3→2, ones 5→8 → answer: **28**
- **45 − 6**: tens 4→3, ones 5→9 → answer: **39**

The pattern is clean, consistent and visual. Children don't count back — they apply a rule.

## What's inside the app

**Learning page — digit box animation**
The question is shown at the top (e.g. 14 − 09). Below it, two large separate digit boxes show the tens and ones of the number being subtracted from. Press Show Me and:
- A **−1** indicator appears above the tens box, which rolls **downward** to its new value
- A **+1** (or +2, +3, +4) indicator appears above the ones box, which rolls **upward** to its new value
- The slot-machine style roll makes the Down-Up movement literally visible

After the animation, the child types the full answer and checks it. Correct answers unlock the next question. Wrong answers prompt a retry.

**Flash Test**
The question flashes on screen for 1, 2 or 3 seconds (child's choice) then disappears. The child types the answer from memory. Ten questions, scored out of 10, with a congratulations screen on completion.

**Progression system**
Children begin with −9 and unlock −8, −7 and −6 as they complete each Flash Test — building confidence step by step, always moving forward.

**Designed for ages 5–7 and children with learning difficulties**
Large text, big tap targets, minimal clutter, clear instructions, and a warm coral/red colour scheme that instantly distinguishes it from the addition companion. No ads. No distractions.

## How to use it

The app is a single HTML file. No installation required. Open it in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or interactive whiteboard. Works offline once downloaded.

- **At home**: a parent and child can work through it together
- **In the classroom**: display on a whiteboard and lead the class through each stage
- **Independent practice**: children can work through it on their own once they know how
